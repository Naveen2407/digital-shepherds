About Digital Shepherds

Digital Shepherds Limited is a private limited company registered in England and Wales, incorporated on 8 April 2025. Our registered nature of business is information technology consultancy.

The company was founded by Naveen Richard Jeyaprakash (Naveen R J) and is based in Outwood, Wakefield. We work as an independent adviser, so our recommendations are shaped by what fits your organisation, its people and its budget, not by a product we need to sell.

A shepherd guides, watches over and keeps things moving safely in the right direction. That is how we approach AI Transformation: practical guidance, careful oversight of engineering and delivery, and a steady focus on getting you where you need to be.

Services

Three core services that take AI from ambition to working outcomes. Each engagement starts with understanding where you are now and where you want to be.

  • AI Transformation consultancy

    Independent advice on where AI can add real value to your organisation: identifying and prioritising use cases, shaping a realistic roadmap, assessing your current systems and data, choosing the right tools and partners, and adopting AI responsibly with the right governance.

  • Software engineering leadership

    Experienced leadership for the teams building your products and platforms: setting technical direction and architecture, strengthening engineering practices, and helping teams build and integrate AI-enabled software well.

  • Delivery leadership and management

    Hands-on leadership to keep technology and AI programmes on track: planning, governance and delivery assurance, managing risks and dependencies, and practical problem-solving when things get complicated.
